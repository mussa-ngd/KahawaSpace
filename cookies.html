Cookie Policy

Understanding how and why we use cookies and tracking technologies on Kahawa Space

Last Updated: October 2026
Effective Date: March 28, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, or mobile device when you visit kahawaspace.com. They enable our platform to recognize your device, maintain session states, remember user preferences, and deliver a smooth browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and session tokens) for several key purposes:

  • Essential Platform Operation: Keeping you logged in, persisting your cart items, and processing secure transactions.
  • Performance & Analytics: Understanding site traffic, user navigation patterns, and feature engagement to continuously improve our marketplace and educational tools.
  • User Preferences: Storing your selected country (which sets the display currency), weight units, language, and theme choices.
  • Marketing & Social Media: Measuring campaign effectiveness and delivering relevant coffee origin updates or marketplace promotions.

3. Categories of Cookies We Set

Cookie Name Category Purpose Duration
session_id Essential Authenticates user session and keeps you logged in across pages. Session
cart_items Essential Saves items selected in your shopping cart before checkout. 30 days
kahawa_preferred_country / kahawa_preferred_currency Preference Saves your country and its corresponding display currency. Until cleared
_ga / _gid Analytics Google Analytics cookie used to distinguish web users and collect aggregated usage stats. 2 years
kahawa_cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie banner choices and consent timestamp. Existing cookie_consent preferences are migrated when you save new choices. 1 year

4. Managing Your Cookie Choices

You have full control over non-essential cookies. You can adjust your consent anytime using our preference center located on our Privacy Policy page, or by modifying settings directly in your web browser:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Prevent cross-site tracking / Block all cookies
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Site permissions > Cookies and site data
