How Kahawa Space Works

Your end-to-end guide to sourcing, learning, and connecting on the world's coffee platform.

1

Create Your Account

Join as a Buyer, Seller, or Coffee Lover. Buyers and sellers complete quick verification to unlock trade features.

2

Discover Coffee & Content

Browse green coffee lots, roasted beans, equipment, courses, and origin tours. Prices display in your local currency.

3

Connect & Negotiate

Message verified sellers directly, request samples, and negotiate terms through the marketplace.

4

Pay Securely

Complete transactions via M-Pesa mobile money or card. Escrow protection keeps both sides safe.

5

Ship & Track

Sellers ship with tracking. Buyers confirm delivery to release payment. Customs guidance included.

6

Learn & Grow

Take expert-led courses, earn certifications, and join tours to deepen your coffee expertise.
