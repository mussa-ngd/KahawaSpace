Privacy & Cookie Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information — and your cookie choices

Last Updated: October 2026
Effective Date: March 28, 2026

Jump to: Overview · Data We Collect · How We Use It · Cookies · Preferences · Your Rights · Contact

1. Introduction

At Kahawa Space, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy & Cookie Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, and how you can control the cookies we set on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide when registering an account, booking coffee tours, making a purchase, subscribing to our newsletter, or contacting customer support. This includes your name, email address, phone number, business details, and local preferences.

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, business details (for sellers).
  • Tour and transaction data: tour bookings, purchases, invoices, and shipping addresses.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers (via cookies — see §5).
  • Preference data: your selected country and display currency, stored in your browser.
  • Communication data: messages sent via contact forms or support.

Your Privacy Matters: We never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process tour bookings and transactions, manage your user profile, provide personalized coffee recommendations, communicate important updates about your account or transactions, improve our services, and help ensure platform security.

4. Data Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These include encrypted authentication tokens and secure data storage practices.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

Cookies help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our platform, and improve your overall experience. They do not contain personal information unless you have provided it to us.

5.1 How We Use Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function properly (login, shopping cart, checkout).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences (language, currency, region).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our platform and improve performance.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign effectiveness.
  • Security Cookies: Help protect your account and detect suspicious activity.

5.3 Third-Party Cookies

We use trusted third-party services that may place cookies on your device: Google Analytics (traffic analysis), Facebook Pixel (advertising effectiveness), Stripe (payments), Intercom (support chat). These third parties have their own privacy policies.

5.4 Currency Conversion

Currency conversion requests live exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API. The request is made by your browser and may expose your IP address to that provider; your selected country and account details are not included in the request.
